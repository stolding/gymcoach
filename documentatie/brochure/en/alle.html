From machine to plan

GymCoach does the work between two sessions.

The Gym Monster sends every set to the Speediance cloud. GymCoach pulls that in and drafts the next plan.

Smart progression

Hit the top of the range and the weight goes up. Miss it twice and it comes back 10%. Every sixth week is a deload, sooner if recovery asks. A session far from that exercise’s median does not move the strength index.

Fits your week

Each day is strength, circuit or outdoors, with a focus. The week is one plan: an exercise moves to a later day when that muscle has not recovered. A walk or other activity belongs in the same week.

Warm-up and cooldown

Mobility and activation for the muscles of that day, and a cooldown after. Ready to start, not just a list of exercises.

AI coach

Ask about your plan. The answer uses your real numbers: volume, recovery and what you lifted last time. You choose what to keep.

One click to the machine

Review, then send. Delete or replace one session and the rest stays. Exercises stay collapsed until you open them. Order follows the cable position on the Gym Monster.

Body, Health and score

Withings supplies weight, fat and muscle. Apple Health supplies activities, steps, heart rate, HRV and VO2max. A health score from 0 to 10 sits on top.

The week plan3
The editor

Change any suggestion. Or build your own session.

Edit a session from the plan, start empty, copy one or pick a template. The figure and the sets per muscle update immediately.

Every set

Reps, weight, rest and the Gym Monster mode: standard, eccentric, isokinetic, constant or spotter. Pyramid and warm-up set in one click.

Supersets

Two or more exercises alternating, with no rest between them. On the machine they are alternating steps, with rest after each round.

Browse exercises

By muscle and type, with video, cues and an explanation. Fetched once and stored on your server.

Locked, and still moving

An edited session stays when a new plan is built. Weights still follow your progression, except where you pinned the weight.

Share templates

Save a session as a template. On a server with several people, each keeps their own history.

The AI coach watches

Also on a session you built. The structure stays; every suggestion has a reason and you tick what you take.

Warm-up and cooldown are edited the same way. Exercise order follows the cable position, so you are not swapping the cable on every change.

Colour follows the score

Greener when the reading pulls the health score up. A BMI moving toward 22 turns greener even if the number falls. A rising resting heart rate turns redder, because higher is not progress here.

Composition

Fat, muscle and the split across arms, legs and torso. Segments come from the Withings scale once it is linked.

Heart and fitness

Resting heart rate, HRV, VO2max and pulse-wave velocity from Apple Health. Steps and activities from the same iPhone fill the week’s goal.

Not a medical opinion

The score and the colours summarise your own readings, so you can see the direction. They do not replace a doctor.

Body5
Numbers over a longer stretch

Looking back: strength, volume and recovery on one tab.

This tab used to be called Analysis. The same charts, plus print and a PDF of the whole tab.

Strength index

100 is your start. The line is the median of your sessions, so one outlier does not drag the picture. A session more than 15% off that median is left out.

Volume and sets

Weekly totals, per muscle, and whether the week is balanced. A muscle that falls behind shows up in the next plan.

When, and recovery

Which days you trained, how long you recovered, and whether burned energy tracks your weight.

Kilometres and steps

Cardio and the step count beside the weights, from Apple Health. The week as you actually moved.

Print and PDF

The whole tab fits on paper. A block that no longer fits moves entirely onto the next page.

Records

Your heaviest set per exercise, and when it happened. The session report emails the same kind of numbers after each workout.

Looking back6
In your inbox

Your workout report, mailed after every session.

Once the session has arrived, GymCoach builds a PDF and mails it to you. Or you open it in the app.

Headline numbers

Duration, sets, volume, calories and heart rate, plus the figure with sets per muscle.

Compared with last time

Duration, volume, sets and heart rate against the average of the last four times you did the same session.

Time under tension

How much of the session was real work, how much was rest, and how long each rep took.

Heart rate zones

The Apple Watch trace, with minutes per zone, from recovery to max.

Every exercise

Sets, reps, weight and records against last time, and whether the last reps slowed down.

Your own mailbox

On or off per user under Settings → Email. Works with any mailbox you run (SMTP). The report in this brochure is sample data.

After every session7
Men, women, and your own recovery

The same program, different numbers.

Sex, heart rate and recovery set the rest, the sets and the colours. Everyone keeps their own history.

Rest and sets

Women get about 20% shorter rest by default, and an extra set where it fits. Max-heart-rate formulas differ by sex. You can pin the rest yourself.

Your own recovery

After a hard session a muscle waits longer. That is your pattern, not a table from a book. The week moves the exercise to a day when that muscle can work again.

Guide

Tuned to you8
On your own server

No account with us. No subscription.

GymCoach is open source (MIT). The site talks only to your server. Training data is not sent to a third party, except the links you turn on yourself.

Install

git clone, then bash install.sh, then the browser at http://your-server-ip:8765. Node.js 22.5 or newer. Linux. Tested with Gym Monster model 1.

You need

  • A Speediance Gym Monster
  • A Linux server with Node 22.5+
  • A browser on your laptop

Optional

  • A Withings scale
  • GymCoach Sync on iPhone
  • Your own mailbox (SMTP)
  • Claude, or a local model

The iPhone app is read-only: overview, plan, goal and body. Linking Apple Health uses a separate key, not your login. You create the key in GymCoach; on the phone you enter the site address.

Technology9
What you are working toward

A goal for body, strength, steps and activities.

The Goal tab keeps the line. The scale itself stays on Body. Profile and units stay in Settings.

Body

Choose how many kilos to move and in how many weeks. GymCoach draws the straight line and plots your weigh-ins beside it. Fat percentage and muscle can join in if you set them.

Strength by muscle

Up to six muscle groups, with a percentage and a date. The plan picks the exercises that belong to them. You do not set a goal exercise by exercise.

Steps

A target per day. The iPhone supplies the count through Apple Health, with the rest of the daily totals.

Activities

Walking, swimming, padel: how often, how long, and a distance if you want one. GymCoach suggests targets from your last four weeks. Apple Health fills the bar when you have done it.

Intake

The calorie target comes from Mifflin-St Jeor, with your goal worked in, plus a protein amount. Hourly Apple Health values add up to one daily total.

The plan stays yours

The plan can follow your goal when you turn that on. Leave it off and the plan stays as it was; only the chart follows your line.

The arrow is not the goal

A rising number is not automatically good. Colour looks at whether that reading pulls the score up or down. A falling fat percentage turns green; a rising resting heart rate turns red.

On iPhone

A heading per category, an icon per reading, and a table of the trend. Read-only: you change things on the site.

In the app, and on iPhone

An explanation of every screen, with an example.

Settings → Guide. On iPhone the same guide is under More. The pictures are a sample user, so you can see what each block shows.

New: connecting

Withings: an admin creates an app at developer.withings.com, then Link Withings. Apple Health: create a pairing key in GymCoach (it starts with gch_), and on the iPhone enter the site address. That address is not the ingest address.

The screens

Overview, Plan, Goal, Body, Training, Looking back and Settings. Each tab has a numbered page and a close-up of every block, with what you see there.

How it is measured

Progression, recovery, the health score and where a number comes from. The same story as this brochure, next to the buttons you actually use.

Away from home

GymCoach Sync shows overview, plan, goal, body and the score. Editing, mail and the links stay on the site. Off home wifi, health sync can wait if you set it that way.

The guide follows the app language: Dutch, English, German or Norwegian.
