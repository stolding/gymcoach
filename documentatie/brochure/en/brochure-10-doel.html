What you are working toward

A goal for body, strength, steps and activities.

The Goal tab keeps the line. The scale itself stays on Body. Profile and units stay in Settings.

Body

Choose how many kilos to move and in how many weeks. GymCoach draws the straight line and plots your weigh-ins beside it. Fat percentage and muscle can join in if you set them.

Strength by muscle

Up to six muscle groups, with a percentage and a date. The plan picks the exercises that belong to them. You do not set a goal exercise by exercise.

Steps

A target per day. The iPhone supplies the count through Apple Health, with the rest of the daily totals.

Activities

Walking, swimming, padel: how often, how long, and a distance if you want one. GymCoach suggests targets from your last four weeks. Apple Health fills the bar when you have done it.

Intake

The calorie target comes from Mifflin-St Jeor, with your goal worked in, plus a protein amount. Hourly Apple Health values add up to one daily total.

The plan stays yours

The plan can follow your goal when you turn that on. Leave it off and the plan stays as it was; only the chart follows your line.
