The editor

Change any suggestion. Or build your own session.

Edit a session from the plan, start empty, copy one or pick a template. The figure and the sets per muscle update immediately.

Every set

Reps, weight, rest and the Gym Monster mode: standard, eccentric, isokinetic, constant or spotter. Pyramid and warm-up set in one click.

Supersets

Two or more exercises alternating, with no rest between them. On the machine they are alternating steps, with rest after each round.

Browse exercises

By muscle and type, with video, cues and an explanation. Fetched once and stored on your server.

Locked, and still moving

An edited session stays when a new plan is built. Weights still follow your progression, except where you pinned the weight.

Share templates

Save a session as a template. On a server with several people, each keeps their own history.

The AI coach watches

Also on a session you built. The structure stays; every suggestion has a reason and you tick what you take.
