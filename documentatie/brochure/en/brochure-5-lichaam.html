Colour follows the score

Greener when the reading pulls the health score up. A BMI moving toward 22 turns greener even if the number falls. A rising resting heart rate turns redder, because higher is not progress here.

Composition

Fat, muscle and the split across arms, legs and torso. Segments come from the Withings scale once it is linked.

Heart and fitness

Resting heart rate, HRV, VO2max and pulse-wave velocity from Apple Health. Steps and activities from the same iPhone fill the week’s goal.

Not a medical opinion

The score and the colours summarise your own readings, so you can see the direction. They do not replace a doctor.
